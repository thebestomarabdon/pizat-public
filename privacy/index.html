Pizat Help & support

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Who controls your data

Pizat is provided by Omar Abdon, based in Kuwait. Privacy questions can be sent to gander.filler-5o@icloud.com.

What Pizat does

Pizat is a financial-memory assistant. It helps you capture, review, search, and summarize personal transaction records. It does not connect directly to banks, move money, provide credit, or provide investment advice.

Information processed on your device

Pizat can process transaction amount, currency, merchant, category, date and time, notes, payment labels, capture metadata, and the source text you choose to import. It also stores preferences such as home currency, language, onboarding state, and merchant-category memory. These records are kept in Pizat's encrypted local database.

Capture permissions

On Android, notification capture is optional. After you enable notification access, Pizat inspects notification title, text, and subtext locally and retains only signals that appear payment-related. On iPhone, transaction fields enter Pizat only through Shortcuts or App Intents that you configure and invoke. You can use manual and clipboard capture without enabling either platform capture method.

On-device intelligence

Deterministic parsing and bundled on-device models can help interpret capture text. Pizat does not send capture text to a cloud AI service in this Beta.

Currency conversion

When a transaction needs an estimated exchange rate, Pizat requests a rate from ExchangeRate-API using the three-letter source currency code. The request does not include the transaction amount, merchant, raw capture text, target currency, or ledger contents. The rate provider receives ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Optional private-cloud backup

If you create a backup, Pizat compresses and encrypts it on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase. The encrypted artifact is stored in your private CloudKit database on iPhone or your Google Drive app-data storage on Android. Pizat cannot recover your passphrase or read the encrypted backup. Apple or Google processes the account authorization, storage request, and ordinary network metadata under its own terms.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Pizat contains no advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, tracking, data broker, or Pizat-operated user account in this Beta. Pizat does not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local transaction records remain until you delete them or reset local data. A deleted transaction has a short Undo window and is then permanently removed with its linked raw-alert audit rows. Settings includes a guarded action to delete all local Pizat data and restart onboarding. Cloud backups are separate: use Pizat's guarded cloud deletion action to remove them. Resetting local data does not revoke operating-system notification access, Google authorization, or remove your iPhone Shortcuts.

Security and your choices

Pizat uses an encrypted local database and encrypts backups before upload. Keep your device protected and store your backup passphrase safely; no one can restore the backup without it. You choose whether to enable platform capture and whether to create or delete a cloud backup.

Children

The Pizat Beta is intended for adults aged 18 and over in Kuwait who use it for personal spending awareness. It is not directed to children and does not create a financial-services relationship.
