Pizat Privacy policy

Help & Support

Support for the Pizat Beta.

What Pizat supports

Pizat supports manual and clipboard capture, optional Android payment- notification capture, and iPhone capture through configured Shortcuts or App Intents. Pizat cannot read your full Apple Wallet or Google Wallet history and does not connect directly to banks.

Sharing a merchant summary

Review the merchant summary before sharing and check the selected recipient. Cancel the preview to exit before opening sharing options. Opening the sharing options does not confirm delivery. Once shared, copies are managed by the recipient app and are not removed by Pizat's deletion controls.

If a transaction is missing

  1. Open Settings and verify that your intended capture method is set up.
  2. Check the Review queue for uncertain or incomplete captures.
  3. Use manual or clipboard capture when a platform signal is unavailable.
  4. For foreign currency, confirm that the original amount and currency are present; Pizat will show the base-currency conversion or a retry state when a rate is unavailable.

Backup and restore

Backups are optional. Their contents are encrypted with your passphrase before upload; operational metadata, such as backup dates and app versions, remains outside that encryption. Pizat cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. A local-data reset does not automatically delete your cloud backup; use the separate cloud deletion control when required.

Delete your data

Delete individual transactions from their detail screen. To remove all local Pizat data, use the guarded Delete all data action in Settings. To remove the encrypted cloud artifact, use the separate cloud-backup deletion action.

Beta limitations

  • Capture depends on information exposed by your device and setup.
  • Exchange rates are estimates unless you enter an issuer-posted amount.
  • The first Beta is English-first; Arabic coverage is still expanding.
  • Pizat is not a bank, payment service, or accounting system.

Report a problem

Email gander.filler-5o@icloud.com. Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, the screen or capture method involved, and steps to reproduce. Do not send bank credentials, complete card numbers, backup passphrases, signing keys, or unredacted financial notifications.

We aim to acknowledge ordinary requests within 5 business days and urgent privacy or security reports within 48 hours.

Privacy or security concerns

Use the same monitored address with the subject “Privacy” or “Security”. Omar Abdon is the incident owner. Do not attach secrets or unredacted transaction data.
